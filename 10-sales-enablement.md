# 10 — Sales Enablement

## One-page battlecard

### What is FrameFlow?

An AI visual-content workflow for e-commerce teams that need to produce consistent product imagery across channels.

### Who is it for?

Brands with high SKU volume and a creative-production bottleneck.

### What problem does it solve?

Repeated image editing, versioning, approval and channel adaptation.

### Why now?

AI generation has lowered the cost of creating visual variants. The next bottleneck is making that production repeatable and controllable.

## Discovery questions

1. How many new SKUs go live each month?
2. How many people touch product imagery before publication?
3. How long does one SKU take from source image to approved assets?
4. Which channels require different image formats?
5. What percentage of creative work is repetitive?
6. What happens when an AI-generated visual changes the product?
7. Where do assets and brand rules live today?

## Qualification signal

Strong fit if the prospect has:
- High visual volume
- Repeated production work
- Multiple channels
- Clear brand standards
- Willingness to run a measurable pilot

## Demo story

1. Start with the messy input.
2. Apply the rules.
3. Generate the asset set.
4. Review.
5. Scale.

The demo should tell a workflow story—not a feature tour.
