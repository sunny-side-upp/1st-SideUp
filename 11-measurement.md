# 11 — Measurement Framework

## North-star metric

### Activated SKUs per account per month

Why? It combines product usage with the core value proposition: getting more products through the visual workflow.

## Funnel metrics

| Stage | Metric | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Qualified visit rate | Target-account visits / total visits |
| Signup | Signup CVR | Signups / qualified visits |
| Activation | Activation rate | Activated accounts / signups |
| Value | Activated SKUs | SKUs reaching approved asset state |
| Conversion | Pilot→paid | Paid accounts / completed pilots |
| Retention | 30-day active accounts | Accounts with meaningful workflow activity |
| Expansion | SKU growth | Activated SKUs/account over time |

## Experiment example

### Hypothesis

A workflow-led hero will outperform a generic AI-generation hero.

**A:** "Generate beautiful product images with AI."

**B:** "Turn one product photo into every visual your store needs."

**Primary metric:** Demo / trial-start conversion.

**Guardrail:** Qualified-demo rate.

### PMM discipline

Do not optimize CTR in isolation.

Ask:

> Did the message attract the right buyer and move them toward product value?
