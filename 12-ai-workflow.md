# 12 — AI Workflow & Guardrails

## Why include this?

AI-tool fluency should be demonstrated through the **process**, not by claiming that AI wrote everything.

The PMM owns the problem framing, audience, strategy, judgement and final quality bar.

## Workflow

1. **Research** — summarize public documentation and generate research questions.
2. **Diverge** — generate multiple positioning directions.
3. **Critique** — attack each direction for differentiation, clarity, credibility and missing proof.
4. **Decide** — human PMM chooses the strategic direction.
5. **Draft** — use AI selectively for variants, outlines or copy exploration.
6. **Verify** — check factual claims against primary sources.
7. **Edit** — rewrite for audience, brand voice and clarity.
8. **Test** — use real audience feedback or experiments.

## Guardrails

Never use AI to invent:
- Customer results
- Customer quotes
- Product capabilities
- Competitive claims
- Market statistics
- Regulatory claims

For confidential employer work, do not upload proprietary documents or customer information into consumer AI tools unless explicitly permitted.

## The PMM value-add

AI can accelerate the **production of options**.

It cannot outsource:

**judgement → prioritization → positioning → trade-offs → accountability**
