# 08 — Launch Campaign

## Campaign concept

# ONE PHOTO. EVERY SURFACE.

Show the transformation from a single source image into a complete visual system.

## Launch assets

### Announcement email

**Subject:** One product photo. Every channel.

**Body direction:**

Your catalog shouldn't need a new production workflow every time a product appears somewhere new.

FrameFlow turns existing product imagery into a consistent set of channel-ready visuals—so your team can spend less time repeating production work and more time improving the creative.

**CTA:** See the workflow

### LinkedIn launch post

**Hook:**  
Your creative team shouldn't be the bottleneck between "product ready" and "product live."

**Body:**  
We built FrameFlow around a simple idea: start with the product image you already have, then make the rest of the visual production repeatable.

PDPs. Marketplaces. Paid social. Campaign variants.

One source → controlled workflow → approved assets.

**CTA:** Explore the workflow.

### Sales one-liner

> "FrameFlow helps e-commerce teams turn existing product imagery into consistent, channel-ready visual assets without repeating the production process for every SKU."
