# AI-Assisted PMM Workflow

## How I would use AI

### Research
Use Claude / ChatGPT to turn public product documentation into:
- research questions
- competitor comparison hypotheses
- customer interview prompts

### Ideation
Generate several:
- positioning territories
- headline directions
- campaign concepts
- CTA options

### Critique
Ask AI to challenge:
- differentiation
- clarity
- unsupported claims
- buyer relevance
- conversion friction

### Human judgement
Choose the strategic direction myself.

### Verification
Check product capabilities and competitor claims against primary sources.

### Production
Use AI for copy variants and iteration, while using Figma for the actual visual system.

## Guardrails

I would not use AI to invent:
- customer results
- testimonials
- product capabilities
- market statistics
- competitor claims

For employer work, confidential material should not be uploaded to consumer AI tools without permission.

## Why this matters

The PMM advantage is not "I can prompt."

It is:

**research → judgement → positioning → execution → measurement.**
