# 90-Second Loom Pitch

## Opening — 0:00–0:12

"Hi, I'm Surya. I looked at Crop.photo as a PMM rather than simply as an AI image product, and I found an interesting opportunity around the problem of visual consistency at catalog scale."

## Product insight — 0:12–0:30

"Crop.photo already brings together bulk editing, AI product photography, reusable recipes and retailer-ready outputs. So I wouldn't market it as just another AI image generator. I'd position the workflow around helping ecommerce teams keep a growing catalog visually consistent."

## The idea — 0:30–0:52

"My growth idea is a free Visual Consistency Audit. A brand uploads ten sample SKUs and gets a simple scorecard, three personalized transformations and a recommended workflow. The goal is to let the product demonstrate its value before asking for a sales conversation."

## Growth loop — 0:52–1:08

"The funnel is Search or LinkedIn into the audit, then personalized proof, then a Recipe, then a 50-SKU pilot and finally paid expansion. I'd measure audit conversion, sample generation, activated SKUs, pilot-to-paid and expansion."

## Show the work — 1:08–1:22

"To make the idea tangible, I built the landing page, campaign creative, lifecycle emails and sales one-pager in Figma."

## Close — 1:22–1:30

"The larger PMM principle is simple: don't lead with the feature list. Lead with the workflow problem, prove the value quickly, and build the GTM loop around the product's strongest behavior."
