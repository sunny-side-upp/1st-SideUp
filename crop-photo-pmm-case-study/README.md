# Crop.photo — PMM Growth Sprint

**Role-specific portfolio project by Surya Sharma**

> **Objective:** Show how I would approach Product Marketing at Crop.photo by turning product capabilities into a coherent PLG / demand-generation story.

## The brief

Crop.photo is an AI-powered visual workflow for e-commerce. Its current product story spans bulk image editing, AI product photography, reusable Recipes, retailer-ready exports, Shopify workflows, banners, video and APIs.

Rather than make another generic "AI image generator" campaign, I built a growth concept around a sharper buyer problem:

> **As catalogs scale, visual inconsistency becomes a production and brand problem.**

### My proposed wedge

**The Free Visual Consistency Audit**

A prospect submits a small set of product images and receives:

- a visual consistency score
- product-fidelity / framing / background / channel-readiness observations
- 3 sample transformations
- a recommended Recipe/workflow
- a CTA to run a measurable 50-SKU pilot

The idea turns product value into an acquisition loop:

**Search / social → Audit → Personalized proof → Recipe → Pilot → Paid**

1. Audit complete

3. Recipe / workflow activation

### 5. Sales enablement

A one-page seller narrative turns the audit into discovery:

- SKUs per month

- repetitive production work
- pilot success criteria

## Why I chose this direction

Crop.photo already has strong product capabilities. Public product pages describe bulk processing, reusable recipes, AI product photography, retailer-specific outputs, Shopify integration and APIs. The challenge I see is not "more features"; it is helping the buyer understand the business system those features create.

The proposed narrative is therefore:

**Fix what you have → generate what you need → standardize it → scale it.**

## Measurement

I would validate:

| Stage | Metric |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Audit landing-page conversion |
| Lead quality | Qualified-audit rate |
| Activation | % generating sample transformations |
| Product value | Activated SKUs |
| Sales | Sample → demo |
| Commercial | Pilot → paid |
| Expansion | SKUs / account + workflow adoption |

The north-star hypothesis is **activated SKUs per account**, because it connects usage to the product's catalog-scale value.

## What I would test before launch

1. **Audit vs free trial** as the primary CTA.
2. **Consistency** vs **production speed** as the hero benefit.
3. Personalized sample vs generic product demo.
4. 10-SKU vs 50-SKU pilot framing.
5. Workflow-led landing page vs feature-led landing page.
