Measure the behavior, not the vanity.

Every strategic claim becomes a hypothesis with a metric attached.

WHAT I WOULD TEST NEXT
“Consistency” vs “production speed” as the hero benefit. Personalized sample vs generic demo. Audit vs free trial. 10-SKU vs 50-SKU pilot.
