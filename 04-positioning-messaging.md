# 04 — Positioning & Messaging

## Category decision

I would avoid leading with "AI image generator." That category is crowded and makes the product easy to compare feature-by-feature.

### Proposed category

**AI Visual Content Workflow**

This puts the emphasis on operational value.

## Positioning statement

> For e-commerce teams producing product content at scale, FrameFlow is an AI visual-content workflow that turns existing product imagery into consistent, channel-ready assets. Unlike general creative tools, FrameFlow is designed around catalog volume, brand rules, approvals and repeatable distribution.

## Message hierarchy

### Level 1 — Outcome

**More product visuals. Less production work.**

### Level 2 — How

**Turn one source image into a controlled set of channel-ready assets.**

### Level 3 — Proof mechanisms

- Reusable brand rules
- Batch workflows
- Human approval
- Channel-specific outputs
- Asset history
- Integrations / API

### Level 4 — Features

Only introduce features after the buyer understands the outcome.

## Messaging by persona

| Persona | Headline | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Head of E-commerce | Scale visual merchandising without scaling production overhead. | Volume + workflow analytics |
| Creative Ops | Make the rules reusable, not the work repetitive. | Templates + approvals |
| Merchandiser | Go from product photo to channel-ready asset set. | Guided workflow |

## Competitive framing

### Don't say

"We have better AI."

### Say

"We are designed for the operational reality after the image is generated."

That gives sales a sharper conversation:

**Generation → consistency → approval → distribution**

## Objection handling

### "We already use Photoshop / Firefly."

Great. FrameFlow does not need to replace creative tools. The value proposition is reducing repetitive production and operational handoffs around high-volume product imagery.

### "We already use Photoroom."

The evaluation should focus on workflow fit: catalog rules, approvals, integrations, governance and how the tool fits the team's existing production process.

### "AI can change the product."

Product fidelity should be a first-class acceptance criterion. The workflow should include human review and configurable rejection / regeneration rules before publishing.
