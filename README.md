## The 30-second version

FrameFlow is a **hypothetical B2B SaaS product** designed for e-commerce teams that need to turn one raw product image into a consistent set of PDP, marketplace, paid-social and campaign visuals.

The PMM problem I chose: **AI image generation is getting easier, but scaling visual production is still a workflow problem.** Teams need repeatability, brand control, channel-ready outputs and a way to move from asset creation to publishing without rebuilding the process for every SKU.

This case study shows how I would take that product from **market insight → ICP → positioning → messaging → launch → lifecycle → measurement → sales enablement**.

> **Important:** FrameFlow is fictional. All product claims, targets and business assumptions are clearly labelled as hypotheses. Competitive observations are based on publicly available product information. No confidential employer material is used.

## The core PMM thesis

**Don't sell "AI image generation." Sell the removal of the visual-content bottleneck.**

For an e-commerce team, the pain is not simply that creating an image takes too long. The operational problem compounds across:

**SKU volume → inconsistent source imagery → editing → brand review → channel formatting → campaign variations → approvals → publishing**

FrameFlow is therefore positioned as a **visual content operating layer**, not another prompt box.

### Positioning statement

> For e-commerce teams producing product content at scale, FrameFlow is an AI visual-content workflow that turns existing product imagery into consistent, channel-ready assets at scale. Unlike general-purpose creative tools, FrameFlow is designed around catalog workflows, brand rules and repeatable production rather than one-off image creation.

I deliberately built the work around the PMM chain:

## Selected assumptions

These are **portfolio hypotheses**, not reported company results:

- Initial sales motion: product-led demo + sales-assisted conversion.
- Initial ACV hypothesis: $6K–$18K ARR.
- Initial beachhead: North American and European e-commerce brands.
- Primary acquisition channels: SEO, founder/PMM-led outbound, partner ecosystems and product-led content.
- Activation event: first approved multi-channel asset set generated from a product image.
- North-star operating metric: **Activated SKUs per account per month**.

## Research notes

The competitive landscape already contains products that cover AI product photography, background generation, batch editing, APIs and visual workflows. Photoroom describes batch processing, brand kits, APIs and marketplace workflows; Claid describes ecommerce image automation, APIs and custom image pipelines; Adobe Firefly offers AI-generated product shots with control over lighting, background, angle and composition.

Sources:
- Adobe Firefly: https://www.adobe.com/in/products/firefly/features/ai-photo-editor/product-shot.html
- Claid ecommerce: https://claid.ai/ecommerce
- Claid APIs: https://claid.ai/apis/
- Photoroom: https://www.photoroom.com/ai-product-photography
- Photoroom API: https://www.photoroom.com/api

The strategic opportunity in this case study is therefore **not** "AI can generate product images." It is the hypothetical proposition that a focused workflow layer can make visual production easier to operationalize across channels.
