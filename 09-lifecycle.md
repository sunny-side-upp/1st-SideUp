# 09 — Lifecycle Messaging

## Lifecycle principle

Don't send the same message to every account. Trigger communication from product behavior.

## Journey 1 — New signup

**Trigger:** Account created, no upload.

> Your first visual workflow is one upload away.

CTA: Upload a product

## Journey 2 — First upload, no generation

**Trigger:** Product uploaded.

> Your source image is ready. Now define the visual rules you want to reuse.

CTA: Create your first asset set

## Journey 3 — Generated, not approved

**Trigger:** Asset set generated.

> You have the first version. Review it against your product and brand rules before publishing.

CTA: Review assets

## Journey 4 — Activated

**Trigger:** 10+ SKUs + approved asset set.

> You've proven the workflow on 10 products. Ready to scale it across the catalog?

CTA: Start a larger pilot

## Journey 5 — Dormant

**Trigger:** 21 days without activity.

> Your catalog has changed. Your visual workflow doesn't have to start from zero.

CTA: Process new products

## Experimentation framework

Test:
- Outcome vs feature framing
- "Save time" vs "increase production capacity"
- Single-product CTA vs 10-product pilot
- Social proof vs workflow demonstration
- Email timing

Primary metrics:

**Activation rate → retained activation → paid conversion**
